# 示例与主题适配

## 完整示例：早起学习计划

用户输入：

> 帮我做一张小红书封面，主题是“早起学习计划”，主标题写“5:30 早起自律”，风格要粉紫黏土可爱风。

Prompt 草稿：

```text
A pastel clay-rendered Xiaohongshu cover, 3:4 vertical layout, soft lavender and cream yellow color palette, cute kawaii 3D clay style, rounded cards, soft long shadows, clean and airy background.

Main title in a bold rounded cute sans-serif font, exact text: "5:30 早起自律".
Subtitle, exact text: "我的高效晨间 routine".

Center scene: a cute clay girl sitting at a round desk with an open notebook, a tiny coffee cup, a small lamp and a potted plant, surrounded by soft stars and hearts.
Decorative elements: pastel calendar icon, checklist, alarm clock, soft bubbles.

Composition: sparse stars and plant details in the top quarter; a large high-contrast title in the upper-middle; the desk scene in the middle; subtitle in the bottom quarter. Keep all text inside safe margins and legible at thumbnail size.

Mood: healing, soft, motivating, cozy. High quality, smooth matte clay texture, warm morning light.

Avoid landscape or square composition, photorealism, neon saturation, sharp edges, clutter, tiny unreadable text, misspelled text, watermarks, logos, and copied app interfaces.
```

## 主题适配表

| 内容领域 | 主体视觉 | 可用装饰 | 推荐辅色 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 学习/知识 | 读书小人、笔记本、台灯、咖啡 | 清单、日历、闹钟、星星 | 奶油黄、baby blue |
| 美妆/穿搭 | 镜前角色、化妆品、衣架、包包 | 爱心、蝴蝶结、小镜子 | 柔粉、奶油白 |
| 生活方式 | 喝茶、整理房间、做饭、撸猫 | 盆栽、杯子、气泡、家居图标 | 薄荷绿、奶油黄 |
| 旅行/探店 | 行李箱、地图、咖啡店、甜点 | 定位针、相机、路牌 | baby blue、蜜桃粉 |
| 抽象主题 | 圆角信息卡、黏土头像、简单图表 | 表情、小图标、气泡 | 按主题选择一种低饱和辅色 |

这些是适配方向，不是固定模板。优先响应用户提供的主题、物件和文案。
